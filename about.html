MY APPROACH

How I coach

Science First

Every program is built on evidence-based principles — progressive overload, specificity, and recovery. No trends, no fads.

Individual Design

Your program is built for you specifically — your schedule, your equipment, your weak points, your goals.

Accountability Loop

Weekly check-ins ensure your program stays calibrated to your actual progress. Bad week? We adjust. Good week? We push.

Long-Term Thinking

I coach for sustainability. The goal is a body you can maintain for life — not a 12-week crash that rebounds.
