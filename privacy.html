Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2025  ·  FitWid.fit / I-BLITZ Fitness Club

1. Information We Collect

When you use FitWid.fit or apply for coaching, we may collect: your full name, WhatsApp number, email address, fitness goals, training experience, health information you share, and workout data generated or logged through our platform. We collect only what is necessary to deliver our coaching services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: deliver personalised coaching and training programs, communicate about your program and progress, send training updates and check-in reminders, and improve our services. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Client data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure with access controls. Payment data is processed entirely by Razorpay and is not stored by FitWid.fit. We retain your data for as long as you are an active client and for 12 months after your last interaction, after which it is deleted upon request.

4. WhatsApp Communication

By providing your WhatsApp number through our apply form or booking system, you consent to receive training updates, program changes, check-in reminders, and coaching communications via WhatsApp from Coach Sahil Bansal. You can opt out of WhatsApp communications at any time by messaging "STOP" or notifying us at coach@fitwid.fit.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We use essential cookies for site functionality. We also use Vercel Speed Insights (anonymous, aggregate page performance analytics) and may use Google Analytics in the future. No advertising cookies or tracking pixels are used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to access our content.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations), and withdraw consent for communications at any time. To exercise these rights, email coach@fitwid.fit.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

Firebase (Google) — data storage and authentication
Razorpay — payment processing
Vercel — website hosting and analytics
WhatsApp (Meta) — client communications

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data. However, no internet transmission is completely secure. If you have reason to believe your data has been compromised, contact us immediately at coach@fitwid.fit.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated to active clients via WhatsApp or email. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: coach@fitwid.fit
